## Role

ECHO: HoloMap is the spatial intelligence layer. It shows routes, points of interest, crash sites, relays, hazards, missions, bases, anomalies, and world overlays.

HoloMap should make the world feel readable without removing the mystery of exploration.

## Overlay Types

HoloMap can support:

- Mission route overlays.
- Crash site markers.
- Hazard regions.
- Relay locations.
- Faction-controlled areas.
- Base and shelter markers.
- Anomaly zones.
- Discovered resource sites.

## Developer Integration

Modules should publish map data through overlays, markers, routes, or region providers. HoloMap should decide presentation, while the module owns the meaning and lifecycle of the data.

A marker should include a label, category, state, visibility rules, and enough metadata for future filters or route planning.

## Ashfall Use

Ashfall can use HoloMap for Gridfall routes, orbital debris zones, signal towers, toxic storm regions, faction paths, relay chains, hidden caches, and Nexus anomaly fields.
